Steel & Trust

Common Questions

Frequently
Asked

How long does installation take?

Standard single shutter installations typically take 3–5 hours including concrete work and finishing. Multiple shutters or large industrial doors may require 1–2 days. We always provide a time estimate before starting work.

What materials do you recommend for coastal areas?

For coastal environments, we strongly recommend hot-dip galvanised steel with a powder-coat finish, or stainless steel (SS 316) for maximum salt-air corrosion resistance. We apply additional anti-rust primers and advise annual maintenance inspections.

Can you retrofit a motorised system to my existing shutter?

Yes, in most cases we can retrofit a tubular motor and control system to an existing manual or gear shutter, provided the structure and drum assembly are in good condition. Our technician will assess during the free site visit.

What does the 5-year warranty cover?

The warranty covers all mechanical failures, structural weld defects, spring fatigue, internal assembly adjustments, and motor electronic malfunctions under normal operational usage conditions.

Do you offer annual maintenance contracts after the warranty period?

Yes. We offer Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) that include two scheduled service visits per year, lubrication and adjustment of all moving parts, priority call-out for breakdown repairs, and discounted spare parts. Contact us for AMC pricing.

What is the typical lifespan of your shutters?

Our GI and steel shutters regularly last 15–25 years with basic annual maintenance. Polycarbonate shutters have a typical lifespan of 10–15 years. Motorised components typically last 8–12 years depending on usage frequency.
